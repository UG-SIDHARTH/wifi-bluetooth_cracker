4. Power & Capacitor Notes
  • Standard PCB Antenna NRF24L01 Modules: Draw ~12mA each. Can be powered directly from ESP32 3.3V pin without external capacitors.
  • PA+LNA External Antenna Modules: Draw up to ~115mA peak. Connect a 10µF to 47µF capacitor across VCC and GND of each NRF24L01 module to prevent brownouts.
